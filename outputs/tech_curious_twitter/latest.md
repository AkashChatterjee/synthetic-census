## 3. **AI's Ground-Truth Impact on Specific Workflows & Labor**

**Why it resonates:** This crosses professional boundaries and generates both professional value and narrative power.

**What they want:**
- Ethnographic detail about how specific jobs are actually changing
- Not "AI will transform X" but "here's what happened to 200 customer service workers over 18 months"
- Workflow friction that makes technically-sound tools fail in practice
- Cognitive/social effects emerging in real communities

**Who's hungry for this:**
- Science Fiction Authors (material for near-future fiction)
- High School Teachers (understanding what's happening to adolescent cognition)
- Newsletter Writers (the human stories readers remember)
- Management Consultants (the organizational dysfunction that kills deployments)
- Policy Researchers (the displacement story becoming a political crisis)

**Key insight:** Everyone has theories. Almost nobody has ground truth from the messy middle. The person who consistently delivers specific, reported stories about how AI is *actually* changing work becomes the bridge between abstract capability and lived reality.

## Strategic Observation

These three topics share a common thread: **they require access, reporting, and honesty that's rare in AI discourse**. The barrier to entry isn't technical knowledge—it's willingness to show receipts, name failures, and resist hype. That's why multiple high-value personas will "drop everything" to read this content.
